## 💡 About

**Smart Calculator** is a free, friendly, all-in-one online calculator built for everyday life. Open it on your phone, tablet or computer and get a clear answer in a second, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Everyday calculations should be simple, fast and free for everyone. Whether you are a student checking an exam cut-off date, a family planning an anniversary, or someone counting the days to a birthday, Smart Calculator is made to save your time and make your day easier.

Every calculation runs **inside your own browser**, so it is quick and your information stays with you.

> **Our promise:** always free, no sign-up, your details stay private, and a smooth experience on mobile and desktop in both light and dark mode.

## ✨ Features

| Calculator | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🎂 **Age Calculator** | Exact age in years, months and days, plus total weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds, your birth weekday and a countdown to your next birthday |
| 💍 **Marriage Calculator** | How long you have been married, next anniversary countdown and the milestones already reached |
| 🕊️ **Death Calculator** | A calm, respectful tool to find the life duration of a loved one for family records and remembrance |
| 📅 **Date Calculator** | Exact gap between two dates, or a new date after adding or subtracting years, months, weeks and days |
| 🗓️ **Calendar** | Clean monthly calendar with weekday and leap year information for any date |
| 🧮 **Advanced Calculator** | Full scientific calculator with brackets, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms, factorial and history |

<details>
<summary><b>Click to see more detail on each calculator</b></summary>

<br/>

**Age Calculator**
Enter your date of birth as numbers (for example `25122005`) and get your age broken down into years, months and days. It also shows the same age in total weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds, tells you the weekday you were born on, and counts down the days left for your next birthday.

**Marriage Calculator**
Enter your wedding date to see how long you have been married, when your next anniversary falls and how many days remain. Milestones you have already crossed are listed too.

**Death Calculator**
Built with care for family records and remembrance. Enter the two dates to find the exact life duration of a loved one.

**Date Calculator**
Two modes: find the exact gap between two dates, or add and subtract years, months, weeks and days from a date to get a new date.

**Calendar**
A clean monthly view for any date, with the weekday and leap year information.

**Advanced Calculator**
A scientific calculator supporting brackets, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms and factorial, with a history of your recent calculations.

</details>

## 🧭 How to Use

1. Open the **[live site](https://abhay2832.github.io/Smart-Calculator/)**.
2. Choose a calculator from the menu.
3. Type your date as numbers, for example `25/12/2005`.
4. Press **Calculate** and the answer appears instantly.
