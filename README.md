## About

**Smart Calculator** is a free, friendly, all-in-one online calculator built for everyday life. Open it on your phone, tablet or computer and get a clear answer in a second, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Everyday calculations should be simple, fast and free for everyone. Whether you are a student checking an exam cut-off date, a family planning an anniversary, or someone counting the days to a birthday, Smart Calculator is made to save your time and make your day easier.

Every calculation runs **inside your own browser**, so it is quick and your information stays with you.

---

## Features

| Calculator | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age Calculator** | Exact age in years, months and days, plus total weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds, your birth weekday and a countdown to your next birthday |
| **Marriage Calculator** | How long you have been married, next anniversary countdown and milestones reached |
| **Death Calculator** | A calm, respectful tool to find the life duration of a loved one for family records and remembrance |
| **Date Calculator** | Exact gap between two dates, or a new date after adding or subtracting years, months, weeks and days |
| **Calendar** | Clean monthly calendar with weekday and leap year information for any date |
| **Advanced Calculator** | Full scientific calculator with brackets, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms, factorial and history |

---

## Why Smart Calculator?

- **Free forever.** No paywall, no hidden charges.
- **No sign-up.** Open the site and start calculating.
- **Private by design.** Calculations happen on your device.
- **Accurate.** Leap years and different month lengths are handled correctly.
- **Responsive.** Works smoothly on mobile and desktop.
- **Light and dark mode.** Comfortable in any lighting.

---

## How to Use

1. Open the **[live site](https://abhay2832.github.io/Smart-Calculator/)**.
2. Choose a calculator from the menu.
3. Type your date as numbers, for example `25122005`.
4. Press **Calculate** and the answer appears instantly.

---

## Run Locally

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/abhay2832/Smart-Calculator.git

# Go to the project folder
cd Smart-Calculator

# Open index.html in your browser
```

No build step and no dependencies are required.
