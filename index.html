About Smart Calculator

What is Smart Calculator? Smart Calculator is a free, friendly, all-in-one online calculator for everyday life. Open it on your phone, tablet or computer and get a clear answer in a second. There is nothing to install, no sign-up and no hidden charges.

Why was it created? Everyday calculations should be simple, fast and free for everyone. Whether you are a student checking an exam cut-off date, a family planning an anniversary, or someone who just wants to know how many days are left for a birthday, Smart Calculator is made to save your time and make your day easier.

What can you do here?

  • Age Calculator: your exact age in years, months and days, total weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds, your day of birth, and how many days are left for your next birthday.
  • Marriage Calculator: how long you have been married, your next anniversary and a countdown, plus the milestones you have already reached.
  • Death Calculator: a calm, respectful tool to find the life duration of a loved one for family records and remembrance.
  • Date Calculator: the exact gap between two dates, or a new date after adding or subtracting years, months, weeks and days.
  • Calendar: a clean monthly calendar with weekday and leap year information for any date.
  • Advanced Calculator: a full scientific calculator with brackets, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms, factorial and history.

How does it work? Just type your date as numbers, like 25122005, and press Calculate. The answer appears instantly, with leap years and different month lengths handled correctly. Every calculation happens inside your own phone or computer, so it is quick and your information stays with you.

Our promise to you. Always free to use. No sign-up. Your details stay private. Works smoothly on mobile and desktop, in light and dark mode. And we keep improving it with your feedback.

Have an idea? If you would like a new calculator or a new feature, please tell us from the menu using Report a Problem or Contact Us. We would love to hear from you.
